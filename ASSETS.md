# Assets — portfolio edition

## Current identity and selected work

The Nurayn logo and all four selected projects use artwork supplied by the user. Files are copied or resized/compressed for web delivery; the underlying project designs and imagery have not been regenerated. CSS provides the surrounding composition, cropping and movement.

- `nurayn.svg`: original SVG supplied from Downloads/Nurayn.svg, preserved unchanged. Its six paths are also embedded in the page with `currentColor` for theme adaptation. The favicon uses the original N path.
- `nurayn-logo.png`: earlier supplied raster logo, retained as source history; no longer displayed.
- `reloop.jpg`: supplied recycling app presentation, optimized to 1440 × 1800.
- `honeydrop.jpg`, `honeydrop-guidelines.jpg`: supplied campaign and guidelines presentations, resized to 1800 pixels wide.
- `honeydrop-mark-dark.png`, `honeydrop-mark-light.png`: supplied monogram expressions.
- `fitness.jpg`, `fitness-portrait.jpg`: supplied fitness app mockups. The website presents this project under the user-authorized working name PACE.
- `little-fixers.jpg`, `little-fixers-repair.jpg`, `little-fixers-success.jpg`: supplied workshop, repair and success presentations. The duplicate repair attachment is not repeated.

Artwork ownership/usage rights are as supplied; no independent rights audit has been performed. Project text describes visible work without asserting unconfirmed commissions or business results.

Fonts: Inter Tight and Instrument Serif from Google Fonts, plus Courier New/system fallbacks.

## Exact generation prompts

### ONDA

Use case: product-mockup. Create a premium art-directed photographic image for a creative studio portfolio concept case study called ONDA, an independent music and listening-culture brand. Landscape 3:2 composition, high resolution. An oversize vermilion-red square record sleeve with very bold black condensed grotesk lettering ONDA, a tactile cream booklet with small disciplined editorial typography, a translucent smoky amber vinyl disc partially pulled from the sleeve, and a polished chrome cylindrical object on a warm ivory photographic studio floor. The record sleeve tilted at an expressive angle, huge confident geometric crop, tight artful arrangement, diagonal hard late-afternoon sunlight and long beautiful shadows. Physical realism, paper grain, subtly scuffed chrome, precision print, museum-quality still-life photography, sophisticated contemporary Swiss graphic design. Palette warm off-white, saturated vermilion, near-black, amber, chrome. The image itself has NO website interface, no UI overlays, no frame, no collage grid, no watermark. Main readable text exactly ONDA. This must feel like a major independent design agency's tactile brand campaign, restrained and striking, not a generic floating product render.

### KANSO

Use case: product-mockup. Create a high-end editorial product photograph for a fictional botanical skincare identity KANSO, landscape 3:2 high resolution. An amber glass pump bottle with a finely printed ivory label reading KANSO in clean black typography and a muted pale-sage paper carton with precise small black type. Place on a weathered sandstone slab, a gently curving sage fabric backdrop and one out-of-focus olive branch casting an elegant shadow. One bottle foreground, carton behind at an offset angle, very close tactile crop. Rich natural warm side light, honest botanical materials, paper grain, glass refraction, no floating objects. Colors muted sage, warm cream, amber, charcoal. Intentional luxury beauty editorial photography with depth, subtle imperfections and elegant asymmetry. This is project artwork for a portfolio, not a website mockup: no web UI, no rounded cards, no added caption overlay, no watermark. Main readable text exactly KANSO.

### FORMA

Use case: product-mockup. Create a bold architectural publishing identity photograph for a fictional independent publisher FORMA. Wide landscape 3:2 high resolution. A heavyweight art monograph with an off-white uncoated cover reading FORMA in huge black tightly tracked grotesk type, a second open book showing full-bleed black-and-white brutalist architecture photography and an elegant serif caption, a folded vivid acidic-yellow poster partly beneath the books. Shot from elevated three-quarter angle on a deep charcoal textured surface, realistic thick paper edges and subtle ink texture. Editorial still-life from a prestigious graphic design studio, dramatic clean light with genuine shadow and depth. Main compositions large and cropped boldly, no generic centered packshot. Color black, cream, one acid-yellow accent. No website UI, no grid collage, no watermark. Exact main cover word FORMA. Actual book and poster physical objects with exceptional typography.
