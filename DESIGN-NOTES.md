# Nurayn — direction and next steps

## The direction

The studio identity stays monochrome, editorial and direct. Your projects supply the colour and personality. One grid, typography system and motion language hold the different sections together.

The SVG wordmark is now the main opening gesture. The pointer-following binary field, bottom navigation and sliding theme transition remain. The longer page adds substance through project imagery, an interactive identity detail, disciplines and process.

## The project edit

1. **Reloop** leads: a clear product story and a distinctive green visual world. It establishes practical interface work immediately.
2. **Honeydrop** follows: the strongest contrast in discipline and mood. Its monogram, warm palette and fashion imagery support a dedicated editorial feature.
3. **Little Fixers** shows playful interaction thinking and a visible three-step journey. It is labelled a concept, as supplied.
4. **PACE** gives the fitness app a concise working name and demonstrates a contrasting light/dark product system.

The website gives these projects the space and presentation of substantial work without inventing client relationships, launch status, testimonials or performance claims.

## Could it win an Awwwards award?

My assessment: this edition is a stronger portfolio foundation, but I would not yet treat it as a Site of the Day submission. Adding sections and animation alone does not make it award-ready.

[Awwwards' published evaluation](https://www.awwwards.com/about-evaluation/) weights design at 40%, usability at 30%, creativity at 20% and content at 10%. The next gains should come from more distinctive storytelling, complete project content and careful execution across devices.

Priorities for the next pass:

1. Build full Reloop and Honeydrop stories from the actual brief, role, decisions, screens and deliverables. Use only confirmed outcomes.
2. Develop one signature interaction that grows from Nurayn's own identity. The current binary, reveal and studio tools are useful starting points; more effects everywhere would dilute the work.
3. Tune rhythm, typography and image transitions with the final content in place. Give the strongest project moments room to breathe.
4. Complete real-device, keyboard, contrast, reduced-motion and performance checks, then confirm contact details and publish.
